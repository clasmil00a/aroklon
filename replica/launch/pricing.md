## Pricing Model

**Free for all features.** TokoKasir is completely free — no subscription, no per-seat fee, no paywalled features.

**Rationale:** The original's biggest complaint was paywalls locking basic features (remote stock updates, etc.) behind a "Pro" tier. Making everything free removes this friction entirely.

**Monetization (optional, future):** If monetization is ever needed, it would be through optional paid services (cloud backup, priority support, multi-location sync) — never by locking core POS features.
