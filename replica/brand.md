## Step 4: Logo Brief

**Idea:** A warm terracotta square with a simple cash register or shop front silhouette — suggests "local shop" and "fast checkout."

**Mark type:** Symbol plus wordmark (symbol for app icon, wordmark for signage)

**Requirements:**
- Must work at 16px (favicon) and 1024px (app icon)
- Deliverables: SVG, app icon 1024x1024 (no transparency for iOS), favicon set, social image 1200x630
- Must NOT resemble Aronium's mark: no shared shape, color pair, or letterform trick
- Place Aronium's logo next to drafts and verify no similarity

## Step 5: Voice

**Three words:** Warm, Direct, Honest

- Warm, not saccharine — friendly but professional
- Direct, not blunt — clear without being harsh
- Honest, not boastful — truthful about capabilities

**Do / Don't pairs:**

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Use "Anda" (formal you) | Use "kamu" (informal) |
| Say "Tidak tersedia" (not available) | Say "Error" or "Gagal" |
| Say "Stok habis" (out of stock) | Say "Item unavailable" |
| Say "Simpan" (save) | Say "Submit" |
| Say "Batal" (cancel) | Say "Abort" or "Hentikan" |
| Say "Berhasil" (success) | Say "Done!" or "Great!" |
| Say "Memuat..." (loading) | Say "Please wait..." |
| Say "Peringatan" (warning) | Say "Caution!" |
| Say "Hapus" (delete) | Say "Remove permanently" |
| Say "Cari..." (search) | Say "Find items..." |

**10 most-seen strings rewritten in voice:**

| Context | Original (Aronium-style) | New Voice (Aroklon/TokoKasir) |
|---------|-------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Sign up button | "Create Account" | "Daftar" |
| Empty cart | "Your cart is empty" | "Keranjang masih kosong" |
| Main action | "Submit Order" | "Simpan Pesanan" |
| Confirm deletion | "Are you sure you want to delete?" | "Yakin ingin menghapus ini?" |
| Error generic | "An error occurred" | "Terjadi kesalahan" |
| Success save | "Saved successfully!" | "Berhasil disimpan" |
| Loading | "Please wait..." | "Memuat..." |
| Warning low stock | "Low stock warning" | "Peringatan: stok rendah" |
| Delete button | "Delete Item" | "Hapus Item" |
| Search placeholder | "Search items..." | "Cari produk..." |
